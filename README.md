# ⚖️ The Tiebreaker

> **Rational Choice & Collaborative Deadlock Resolution Engine**

**The Tiebreaker** helps you break through indecision using multi-framework AI analysis and real-time team consensus tools.

### ✨ Features
* **AI Multi-Criteria Analysis:** Automatically evaluates dilemmas across weighted comparison matrices, quantified pros/cons, and 2x2 SWOT quadrants.
* **The AI Tiebreaker Verdict:** Generates conditional decision rules, identifies hidden blind spots, and alerts you to potential cognitive biases.
* **Collaborative Voting Rooms:** Real-time team rooms supporting 100-point budget allocations, ranked-choice voting, and crowdsourced feedback.
* **Devil's Advocate Mode:** Performs pre-mortem stress testing on your top choices to reveal potential failure modes before you commit.
